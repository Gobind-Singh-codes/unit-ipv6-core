## 2. Design notes

- **SLAAC-01 timing is strict, not choreographed.** Bringup, first-TX, and teardown
  timestamps are same-clock in the JSON (`t_bringup`, `t_first_tx`,
  `window_gap_s`); first stimulus outside `--dad-delay` is INCONCLUSIVE by rule
  ("missed the delay window"), even with a log hit. Bringup failure aborts
  before TX; teardown failure warns without touching the verdict.
- **Known footnote:** the DUT log line shows the address without the `tentative`
  flag (DAD completes during the capture windows before the read). The window
  assertion, not the flag, carries the during-delay proof: stimuli provably left
  at bringup+~0s.
- **Stimuli to the tentative address use next-hop L2** (tentative answers no
  solicitations, so neighbor resolution can never succeed).
- **SLAAC-03/05 deliberately duplicate ND-04/05 wire behavior** with independent
  IDs and verdicts per the duplicate-requirements rule.
